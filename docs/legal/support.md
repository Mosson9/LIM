## English

### What is {{APP_NAME}}?
An anti-consumerism companion. Before you buy something you’re unsure about, you
ask LIM; an AI weighs it across six dimensions (need, alternatives, emotion,
long-term value, cost, environment), shows a 0–100 **impulse index**, and gives a
gentle verdict — keep it, sleep on it, or skip it. Resisting becomes savings and
a growing tree.

### Getting started
1. Create an account with your email.
2. Answer four quick questions (income, household, budget) so advice fits you.
3. Tap the ✦ button and describe what you want to buy.

### FAQ

**How many AI consultations do I get?**
Free accounts get 3 per day. LIM Plus is unlimited.

**Is my financial information safe?**
Your profile is used only to personalize advice. Data is encrypted in transit,
passwords are hashed, and we don’t use ad/tracking SDKs. See our
[Privacy Policy]({{PRIVACY_URL}}).

**What is the 24-hour cooling-off list?**
Items you’re tempted by can be parked in your wishlist for a day. When the timer
ends, decide again — many impulses pass overnight.

**How do I change the app icon?**
Me → 更换 App 图标 / Change App Icon. Some icons are LIM Plus-only.

**Managing your LIM Plus subscription**
- Prices: ¥18/month or ¥98/year (auto-renewing).
- Manage or cancel: iPhone **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**. Cancelling
  keeps Plus until the end of the current period.
- **Restore purchases:** open the Plus screen and tap “Restore”.
- Payment is charged to your Apple ID; it renews unless cancelled ≥24h before the
  period ends.

**Edit my profile / data**
Edit anytime in **Me → basic info**.

**Delete my account and data**
Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your account address with “Delete my account”; we
remove your data within 30 days (excluding records we must keep by law).

**Export my data**
Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to request a copy of your data.

### Troubleshooting
- **Can’t sign in:** check email/password; if you hit “too many attempts”, wait a
  minute and retry (a rate limit protects your account).
- **Data looks out of date / offline:** LIM shows your last-synced data when the
  network is unavailable; it refreshes automatically when you’re back online.
- **Replay the intro:** Me → 重新观看引导 / Replay onboarding.
