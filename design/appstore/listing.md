
**Description** (≤4000)
```
We’re surrounded by too many “wants.” LIM sells nothing — it simply pauses with you the moment you’re tempted but unsure you really need it, so you can think clearly before you decide.

■ Ask LIM before you buy
Tell LIM what you want, roughly how much it costs, and why. Drawing on your income, household and budget, the AI weighs it across six angles:
· Need — is it truly necessary?
· Alternatives — do you already own one, or could you rent, share or repair?
· Emotion — genuine desire, or ads, social pressure and a passing mood?
· Long-term value — how often, how long, will it really last?
· Cost — does the price fit your budget without crowding out what matters?
· Planet — do its making, shipping and use sit right with your values?

■ One clear Impulse Index
The six scores fold into a 0–100 impulse index and a gentle, non-judgmental verdict: keep it / sleep on it / maybe skip it.

■ A 24-hour cooling-off list
Still want it? Park it in your wishlist for a day. Most impulses pass after a night’s sleep — we’ll remind you to decide again.

■ Restraint you can see
Every time you resist becomes real money saved and a slowly growing tree. Track spending, monthly trends, and the categories worth reining in.

■ LIM Plus (optional subscription)
Unlimited AI consultations, faster responses, 9 themed app icons and exclusive growth skins.

LIM believes less is more — not “don’t spend,” but spend with clarity. Put your money toward what truly matters, and save your wants for what’s worth it.

—
Subscription: LIM Plus auto-renews; cancel anytime in Settings.
```
> ~1480 chars
